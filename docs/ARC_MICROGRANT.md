# Arc Microgrant narrative

## One-line description

Arc-native market infrastructure that lets creators launch USDC-settled markets
and route a transparent share of trading fees to NFT communities.

## What Triplfun changes

Most blockchain onboarding asks a user to bridge before giving them a reason to
care. Triplfun is designed to reverse that order. An NFT holder first discovers
that a collection they already own is connected to a claim, then comes to Arc
to verify and use it.

The individual amount can be small. The opportunity is repeatable distribution:
each participating collection can introduce an existing community to Arc,
USDC, and an onchain market through a concrete action rather than a generic
incentive campaign.

## What is live

- Public Arc mainnet application.
- USDC-settled Uniswap v4 launches and trades.
- Immutable launch-time fee configuration.
- Separate creator, holder, treasury, and NFT accounting.
- Event-backed indexing and direct contract checks before wallet writes.
- Public mainnet launch, trading, fee, claim, and NFT mint receipts.

## What comes next

1. Launch the first public NFT-community fee campaign on Arc.
2. Publish its immutable eligibility snapshot and claim evidence.
3. Record the full user journey from NFT eligibility to an Arc USDC claim.
4. Test the onboarding loop with an external community.
5. Design cross-chain snapshot authorization without implying that it already
   exists in production.

## Use of the microgrant

The grant would support the first public community pilot, production evidence,
holder-facing explanation, and documentation of the trust model. The goal is a
small, verifiable campaign that shows whether NFT-linked claims can bring new
users to Arc.
